I changed `parseValue` in `parser.go` so a `#` inside double quotes stays part of the value. Quoted values are now scanned character by character. `\"` and `\\` are unescaped, and the closing quote may be followed only by whitespace or a `# ...` comment. Unquoted values still end at the first `#`.

One behaviour change you didn't ask for: text after a closing quote that isn't a comment, such as `"x" y`, now gives an "unexpected text after quoted value" error. Before, that input gave "unterminated quoted value".
